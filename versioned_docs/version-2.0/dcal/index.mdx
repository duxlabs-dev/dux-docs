# Dux Calendar

Dux Calendar is a free, open-source desktop calendar app for Linux and FreeBSD. Its purpose is to bring your Local, [Google](https://calendar.google.com/), [Microsoft](https://outlook.com/), [CalDAV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CalDAV), and [iCloud](https://www.icloud.com/calendar/) calendars together in one place, so you can view and manage events, respond to meeting invitations, track tasks, and get reminders across all your accounts — while your data stays on your device. It runs as a lightweight daemon with a tray icon, keeps your accounts in sync in the background, and has the look and feel of [Dux Shell](https://github.com/duxlabs-dev/dux-shell). `dcal` is Dux Calendar's command-line name.

Built with [Quickshell](https://quickshell.org/) and [Go](https://go.dev/).

## Features

- **Multiple Providers**: Connect Local, Google, Microsoft, CalDAV, iCloud, Evolution, and read-only iCal/webcal subscriptions, and view every calendar in a single unified agenda.
- **Month, Week, Day, Agenda & Tasks**: Switch between five views, with recurring-event support and full recurrence editing.
- **Tasks**: View, create, and complete VTODO tasks from calendars that provide task lists.
- **Meeting Invitations**: Accept, decline, or tentatively respond to invitations, with attendee status pushed back to your provider.
- **Background Sync**: A lightweight daemon keeps your accounts in sync and serves the UI over IPC. Closing the window only hides it. Sync and reminders keep running.
- **Event Reminders**: Native desktop notifications for upcoming events, with per-event and per-calendar reminder configuration.
- **Tray Integration**: A system tray icon to show, hide, and quit the app, plus quick access to upcoming events.
- **Secure Credentials**: OAuth tokens and account secrets are stored in your system keyring, not in plaintext config files.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full keyboard-driven navigation with a shortcuts overlay, a go-to-date jump, and spotlight-style search across your events.
- **Scriptable IPC**: A scriptable IPC surface for keybinds and automation.
- **Localized**: User-facing strings are translatable and managed through POEditor, with automatic right-to-left layout mirroring.

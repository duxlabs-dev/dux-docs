# Island

The island replaces a bar's center section, or floats on its own as the dot. Its settings live under **Settings → Bar**: the widget list on **Widgets**, the rest in the island cards on **General** and **Appearance**.

## Island widgets

**Settings → Bar → Widgets → Island widgets** works like the left and right sections: drag to reorder, switch a widget off to hide it, click it for its settings, or remove it with **X**. The clock stays and anchors the list. Widgets above it sit before the clock in the island, the rest after it.

**Add widget** offers two groups:

- **Island activities**: media or launcher, weather, control center or battery, volume, brightness, notifications, privacy, workspaces, keyboard layout and tray, built for the island and opening its own views. A removed one comes back from here.
- **Bar widgets**: regular bar widgets and plugin widgets, drawn without their bar pill. Widgets the island already rebuilds, like the clock or battery, are not offered twice.

The dot has no room for bar widgets, so it only offers island activities.

## Dashboard tab

**Dashboard opens to** in the island's behavior card picks the dash tab that clicking or hovering the home face expands into. It starts at Overview.

## Media face

The island's behavior card sets what the collapsed media face shows:

- **Keep clock with media** keeps the clock beside the track.
- **Playback controls** adds previous, play or pause, and next buttons. It is on by default; turn it off to give the title the room.
- **Lyrics** shows the line being sung instead of the title and artist while synced lyrics are available.
- **Switch to media when playback starts** moves the island to the media face when something starts playing.

The dot does not offer these settings.

## Connected popouts

**Connected popouts** in the island's **Satellites** card opens popouts from satellite widgets joined to their satellite, on its background. It needs **Show** and **Background** on with an opacity above 0, and is unavailable while the Frame is on or the island uses the overlay layer.

## Workspace switches

**Workspace Switch** in the island's notifications card shows workspace changes in the island instead of the workspace OSD. It travels as a live activity from the `workspace` source, so it needs **Plugin activities** on and `workspace` left out of **Hidden activity sources**. Without them the toggle is unavailable and the regular OSD shows.

## Live activities

Plugins and `dms ipc call islandActivity` publish activities into the island. The **Live activities** card turns plugin activities on or off, sets how long a new one previews and how many stay as slots, and hides sources. **Activity sources** overrides where one source sits, what it shows collapsed and what a click opens. See [Island Live Activities](./plugin-development#island-live-activities) and the [`islandActivity` IPC target](./keybinds-ipc#islandactivity).

A notification rule's **Island summary** option shows matching notifications in the island with an icon and a title template, while the notification center keeps the original. The template takes `{summary}`, `{body}` and `{appName}`.

## Motion

**Spring** in the island's motion card picks how the island expands and collapses:

| Preset | Stiffness | Damping | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smooth (default) | 600 | 49 | Settles without overshoot |
| Balanced | 560 | 37 | A slight overshoot |
| Playful | 640 | 30 | A visible bounce |

The stiffness, damping and mass sliders below it fine-tune the spring; values that match no preset leave none selected. Reset returns to Smooth. **Reduce motion** turns the spring off.

# Battery

The battery widget shows the system battery and, if wanted, the batteries of connected peripherals. Its options, including the popout's, are under **Settings → Bar → Bar widgets** (click the battery widget).

## Bar widget

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Color** | **Theme**, **Low and charging** (default: red when low, green while charging, accent color while plugged in) or **Level** |
| **Separator** | **Gap** (default), **Line**, **Dot** or **Separate** (each entry gets its own background) |

**Low threshold** opens the alerts in **Settings → Power & battery → Battery**, where the threshold used by **Only when low** is set.

### Entries

**Entries** is an ordered list of the batteries the widget shows: drag to reorder, switch an entry off to hide it. The system battery is in the list too. On a desktop without a battery it is shown as a power icon. Saved devices that are not connected stay in the list as **Not connected** until removed with **Remove**.

Peripherals like mice, keyboards or headsets are not shown by default. Switch one on in the list to add it to the bar. A device that reports 0% is shown as unknown and never counts as low.

For a peripheral, turn on any of **Show icon**, **Show percentage** and **Only when low**. The last one shows the entry only at or below the low threshold. Each entry's options open with the arrow at the end of its row.

The system battery has these options:

- **Only when low** (same as above)
- **Show percentage** (on by default), and **Only on battery** to hide it while plugged in
- **Show remaining time**, and **Only on battery** for the same
- **Show charge rate** and **Show discharge rate** show the power in watts
- **Battery style**: **Icon** (default), **Solid** or **Circle**
- **Direction**: **Horizontal** or **Vertical**

With a charge limit set in **Settings → Power & battery → Battery**, the remaining time while charging is the time until the limit is reached, here and everywhere else it is shown.

## Popout

The **Popout** card sets the look of the whole popout:

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Color** | **Primary** (default), **Theme**, **Low and charging** or **Level** |
| **Interface density** | **Inherit** (default: follows **Interface density** in **Settings → Interface style**), **Spacious**, **Comfortable**, **Compact** or **Dense** |
| **Cards** | **Separate** (default: the level, power profile and details each have their own card) or **Combined** (all three share one card) |
| **Wavy when charging** | Animates the level indicators while charging (on by default) |

While charging toward a charge limit, the popout shows **Charging to** with the limit and the time until it is reached, and the part of the level indicator past the limit is shaded. This does not apply when **Scale battery level to charge limit** is on.

The popout always shows the battery on top, then the power profile with the battery details below it, then the devices. Each part has its own card (with the Chips layout the details sit in the power profile card) unless **Cards** is set to **Combined**, and **Show** switches it off. The gear in the popout opens the battery settings and the pen opens these widget options.

| Section | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | **Style**: **Compact** (default: the level beside the status, without a card), **Line**, **Ring** or **Card**. **Badge**: **Shape** (default) or **Ring** (a ring showing the level), for **Line** and **Card**. **Layout**: **Grid**, **Compact** (default), **Chips** or **List**. **Details**: any of **Health**, **Temperature**, **Capacity** and **Power** (all but **Temperature** by default) |
| **Power profile** | **Style**: **Circle**, **Segments**, **Buttons** (default) or **Slider** |
| **Devices** | **Layout**: same choices as the overview, plus **Bars** (default: rows filled to the battery level) |

At **Compact** and **Dense** density, **Line** looks the same as **Compact** and is not offered, and **Badge** only shows where a badge is drawn. **Temperature** is only offered when the battery reports one.

The **Devices** section appears once a peripheral with a battery is connected. If the last one disconnects while the popout is open, it shows **No devices** until the popout closes. When the **Overview** section is off or there is no battery, the connected device with the lowest known charge takes its place at the top.
